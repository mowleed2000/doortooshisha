Hosting · 6 min

Hosting a shisha night at home without the faff

A good home session is less about buying every accessory and more about sizing the package correctly, keeping air moving, and not running out of coals at midnight.

Pick the right package

  • 1 Shisha (£50) — solo or a quiet two-person share
  • 2 Shisha (£80) — the default for couples and small flats
  • 3 Shisha (£110) — gatherings where people actually stay a while

Extras that matter

Extra coals (£5) are the unsung hero. An extra head (£30) keeps flavour fresh for longer nights. Juice base (£15) changes the mood more than people expect. LED under-light (£15) is pure atmosphere.

Room setup

Crack a window. Use a stable surface away from curtains. Never leave hot coals unattended. Use a heat-safe dish for spent coals.

Order early enough

Even with 24/7 delivery, Friday nights get busy. Place the order when guests are confirmed, not when someone finally asks “shall we?” at 1am — though yes, we still take those.
